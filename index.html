FOR EVERYONE IN KESENNUMA'S SEAFOOD TRADE

What if the fish you counted on don't arrive?

See what may arrive. Plan what comes next. Fishermen, captains, auctions, markets and processors share expected landings, see a possible shortfall and the evidence behind it, and people choose the response: a bilingual app built for Kesennuma.

01 / THE QUESTION

Can this order be covered?

Orders have deadlines. Stock can be confirmed, but future landings remain uncertain. Gyosoku brings these inputs together while keeping confirmed quantities distinct from expectations, so a possible gap is easier to see.

It is for everyone in the chain: fishermen and captains, auctions and markets, processors, and anyone coordinating supply in Kesennuma. Its purpose is to make the evidence behind a decision clear, not to automate it.

THE PREDICTION BOARD

Make an estimate. Learn from the landing.

A simple board for catch estimates and confidence. Compare your prediction with the actual landing afterward. No money or trading.

Prototype entries stay on this device. Shared submissions are not connected yet, and estimates are unvalidated.

“Can't ChatGPT just do this?”

A chatbot is good with language, but it does not know today's landings in Kesennuma. Gyosoku is a system that collects data and does the sums; the AI is only its guide.

Where we use AI

  • Guiding and drafting the entry form
  • Summarising the weather in two lines
  • Japanese and English phrasing

Where we do not

  • Predicting landings
  • Totals and gap calculations
  • The final decision

Next stage: once Kesennuma's past landing records are available, a model trained on local data (a local model) could be added, after it is tested. Today we do not claim any forecast accuracy, so we do not forecast.

Note: for now, entries are saved on each person's own device. Sharing between people is the next stage.

06 / QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

Does AI predict real landings here?

No. Totals come from the estimates fishermen enter themselves and are not a validated forecast. The AI only guides you through the app and summarises the weather; it does not predict landings.

Are my decisions saved?

Catch entries are saved on this device for now. Feedback form answers are sent to the Gyosoku team. Questions to the AI go to an AI service, and the weather summary uses public forecast data. Please do not type personal details to the AI.

Can I install it as an app?

On iPhone, open the site in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the Chrome menu and choose Install app. It is a web app, so there is nothing to download from the App Store.

What are P10, P50 and P90?

They describe a range of possible landing quantities: a low-side outcome, the median and a high-side outcome. All values here are illustrative, not calibrated probability forecasts.
