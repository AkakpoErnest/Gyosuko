FOR EVERYONE IN KESENNUMA'S SEAFOOD TRADE

What if the fish you counted on don't arrive?

See what may arrive. Plan what comes next. Fishermen, captains, auctions, markets and processors share expected landings, see a possible shortfall and the evidence behind it, and people choose the response: a bilingual app built for Kesennuma.

01 / THE QUESTION

Can this order be covered?

Orders have deadlines. Stock can be confirmed, but future landings remain uncertain. Gyosoku brings these inputs together while keeping confirmed quantities distinct from expectations, so a possible gap is easier to see.

It is for everyone in the chain: fishermen and captains, auctions and markets, processors, and anyone coordinating supply in Kesennuma. Its purpose is to make the evidence behind a decision clear, not to automate it.

THE PREDICTION BOARD

Make an estimate. Learn from the landing.

A simple board for catch estimates and confidence. Compare your prediction with the actual landing afterward. No money or trading.

Prototype entries stay on this device. Shared submissions are not connected yet, and estimates are unvalidated.

05 / QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

Does AI predict real landings here?

The current demo uses static synthetic data and explainable arithmetic. Forecast accuracy has not been validated. A future forecasting system would require real-data validation.

Are my decisions saved?

Activity and offer verification live in browser memory and reset on reload. Your language preference is stored on your device. Decisions are not transmitted to a server.

Can I install it as an app?

On iPhone, open in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android or desktop Chrome, use the browser’s install option or the app’s install button when available. It is an installable web app, with no App Store release.

What are P10, P50 and P90?

They describe a range of possible landing quantities: a low-side outcome, the median and a high-side outcome. All values here are illustrative, rather than calibrated probability forecasts.
